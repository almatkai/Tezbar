## What's new in Tezbar

- After installing an update, Tezbar now opens its own release notes the first time you launch it.
- Release notes are included with the app, so you can read them offline.
- Reopen the notes for your installed version from **Settings → App Updates → What's New**.
- Dismiss the notes with **Got it** or **Escape**. They stay dismissed until your next update.

Fresh installations continue to open onboarding first.
